# Hero Siege Item Editor 2.16.1

For AFK FARM 0.8 (the camp) and 0.9 (the town).

## What's new

- **The town's goods for AFK FARM's camp and town.** An AFK FARM adventurer opens
  golden chests with Basic Keys and crystal chests with Crystal Keys from the camp's
  key rack; the jeweler works from the camp's material stock. AFK FARM 0.9's town
  (fortifications, merchants, trade wagons) stocks the town's goods: keys,
  fragments and shards, tarot cards, materials, dusts, rare consumables, runes,
  gems, jewels and orbs. AFK FARM can now fill its stock from your Vault's
  **AFK Materials**: the amounts you choose leave the Vault (smaller stacks first)
  and go to the camp or town, all at once or not at all.
- **Never taken twice.** Each request carries its own id and is carried out at most
  once. If AFK FARM loses the editor's answer, it asks again and gets the same
  result; if the request never arrived, AFK FARM cancels it, and nothing can be
  taken with that id afterwards.
- Custom-named stacks are never taken. Hero Siege may be running (only the Vault
  database changes, like an AFK transfer). The Vault's history lists each take, and
  History undo does not reach past it.

Everything else is as in 2.16.0. Download the exe and run it; no install.
