# Hero Siege Item Editor 2.15.3

Pairs with ForgePact 1.3.10.

## What's new

- **Tier for forged items.** Under Appearance you can now pick the item's Tier letter:
  C, B, A, S or SS. It is what the tooltip prints and what loot filters use. ForgePact
  1.3.10 applies it in the game.
- **Signature items are SS.** Tyrant's Crown and Headhunter made from the ready-made
  list now come out as SS-tier items.
- **Runes, gems and jewels:** the forge now tells you that forged stats on a socketable
  stay on the item itself and do not carry into a socket. The game rebuilds a socket
  bonus from the jewel's type and seed; use Reroll stats to change that.

Everything else is as in 2.15.2. Download the exe and run it; no install.
