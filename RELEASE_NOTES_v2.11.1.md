# Hero Siege Item Editor v2.11.1

This update adds Hero Siege 7.0.6 support and makes item and stash management
faster.

## What's new

- Max/Best rolls and exact numeric tooltips now support Hero Siege 7.0.6.
- Item tooltips are clearer, two Vault items can be compared side by side, and
  Vault items can have searchable custom names.
- All Shared Stash items—or only one selected tab—can be moved to Infinite
  Vault. Items can be returned automatically or to a selected stash tab.
- Unique, Material, and Socket tabs have one-click fill buttons.
- Torch of Shadows lets you choose its class skill when creating the item.
- Poison Ivy uses its correct four-socket limit, and Add Socket works on
  supported items.
- Small Charms use native random rolls; the confusing Grade labels are gone.

Every save change still creates a backup and is blocked while Hero Siege is
running. Loaded Dice and Overloaded Dice skill selection remains unavailable on
7.0.6 until those separate target tables are verified.
