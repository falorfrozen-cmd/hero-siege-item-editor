# Hero Siege Item Editor 2.15.10

Pairs with [AFK FARM](https://github.com/falorfrozen-cmd/HS-AFK-Expedition) 0.6.4 and ForgePact 1.3.10 or newer.

## What's new

- **AFK FARM rewards land in the Infinite Vault.** Each AFK FARM expedition gets its own category, sorted into rarity stashes. Keys, materials, runes and gems go to **AFK Materials**. Keep the editor open while AFK FARM transfers; your game saves are not touched.
- **Materials stack.** Keys, materials, runes and gems of the same kind merge into stacks of up to 999, both when AFK FARM transfers them and when you press **COMPACT ITEMS**.
- **DISMANTLE for AFK loot.** You see what will happen before you confirm, and a backup of the Vault is kept.
  - Each stash of an AFK category has a **DISMANTLE** button.
  - The category menu has **DISMANTLE BY RARITY…** for the whole category.
  - Satanic and higher equipment is broken down the way the Prospector does it: Satanic Crystal Fragments, or a Dice, Gypsy's or Mallet Fragment for S and SS tier. The fragments go to AFK Materials.
  - Items below Satanic are deleted, because gold cannot be added outside the game.
- **Delete categories and stashes.** **DELETE CATEGORY** (category menu) and **DELETE STASH** (stash header) show what will be removed and keep a backup.
- **Clean up by rarity** deletes every item of the rarities you tick in one category. Items you gave a custom name are kept.
- **Large categories open quickly.** A category with thousands of items opens in a fraction of a second.
- **Miner's Helmet** is added to Item Forge's signature items. Its 4× mining ore and Vein Resonance need ForgePact 1.4.5 or newer.

## Before you update

The first time 2.15.10 opens your Infinite Vault, it upgrades the Vault to a new format after writing a backup. Older editors cannot open the upgraded Vault.

Everything else is as in 2.15.4. Download the exe and run it; no install.
